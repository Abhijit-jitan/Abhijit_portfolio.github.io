Truck Appointment Booking & Process Management

Industry: Manufacturing, Supply Chain & Logistics     Technology: Automation, Messaging Platforms, Workflow Systems     Deployment Type: Cloud + IVR + WhatsApp

Problem Statement

Large manufacturing plants receive raw materials via 300–500 trucks per day. Managing truck arrivals, unloading schedules, and quality checks manually creates congestion, long wait times, and inconsistent material handling.

Without a structured appointment system, it becomes difficult to prioritize compliant drivers, maintain material quality, and enforce operational discipline, leading to inefficiencies and disputes at factory gates.

Objectives

  • Streamline truck appointment booking for raw material delivery
  • Reduce congestion and waiting time at factory gates
  • Ensure quality compliance during unloading
  • Incentivize disciplined and compliant drivers
  • Support both individual farmers and cooperatives

Solution Overview & Approach

Solution Overview:
A centralized appointment booking and process management platform that orchestrates truck arrivals, unloading priority, and quality compliance using rule-based workflows and multi-channel access.

Approach:

  • Enabled appointment booking via WhatsApp chatbot and IVR systems
  • Supported bookings for individual farmers and cooperative groups
  • Validated appointment details against truck and delivery data
  • Tracked arrival time, booking compliance, and material quality checks
  • Prioritized unloading based on predefined reward and compliance rules

Tech Stack

Messaging & IVR: WhatsApp chatbot and IVR systems enabling easy appointment booking for drivers without smartphones.

Backend: Workflow-driven backend managing appointments, validations, priority rules, and unloading sequence orchestration.

Operations Dashboard: Web-based dashboard for plant operators to monitor truck flow, unloading status, and compliance metrics.

Deployment: Cloud-native deployment supporting high throughput and peak load handling during operational hours.

High-Level Architecture

The system follows an event-driven architecture connecting booking channels (WhatsApp/IVR), validation engines, and factory operations dashboards.
