Telecom Permit-to-Work Automation App

Industry: Telecom Infrastructure & Field Operations     Technology: Computer Vision, Mobile AI, Automation     Deployment Type: Mobile + Cloud (Field & Enterprise)

Problem Statement

Telecom tower maintenance, new tower erection, or tower dismantling activities require multiple permits, safety validations, and checklist approvals before work can begin.

These processes are traditionally manual, paper-driven, time-consuming, and highly error-prone, often taking up to 3 hours and involving multiple back-and-forth validations between field workers, contractors, and managers.

Objectives

  • Digitize and automate telecom permit-to-work workflows
  • Ensure worker safety compliance using AI-based validation
  • Reduce approval and validation turnaround time
  • Provide real-time visibility to managers and contractors
  • Mobile app for field-workers to take the images & videos as per the checklist
  • Webapp for contractors/managers to monitor or validate AI-based validation

Solution Overview & Approach

Solution Overview:
Mobile app permit-to-work automation platform that uses location intelligence, computer vision, and AI validation to digitize and accelerate telecom field operations.

Approach:

  • Provided a mobile application for field workers at telecom sites
  • Verified work-site location using GPS to prevent unauthorized site activity
  • Captured contextual data such as weather conditions, lighting, and time
  • Allowed workers to capture images and videos of themselves and team members
  • Validated ID cards, PPE compliance, and safety conditions using AI models
  • Automatically populated checklist responses and submitted them to a web portal

Tech Stack

AI:Detection, Tracking, Video Analytics, GenAI

Mobile Application: iOS & Android App

Backend: FastApi BE

Web Application: React Based webapp
