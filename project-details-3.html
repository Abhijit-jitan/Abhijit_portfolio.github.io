Automated Plank & Pipe Count Platform

Industry: Manufacturing     Technology: Computer Vision     Deployment Type: Mobile & Azure

Problem Statement

Counting large stacks of planks or pipes, often consisting of hundreds or thousands of items, time-consuming, error-prone task in manufacturing yards and warehouses.

Manual counting leads to inaccurate inventory records, operational delays, increased labor effort

Objectives

  • Mobile app to take/upload images & crop images
  • Detect plant & pipe with count
  • Eliminate manual counting errors and delays
  • Provide reliable count results

Solution Overview & Approach

Solution Overview:
Azure based deployment, where mobile app will take the image & send image to do the inference with counting.

Approach:

  • Collected real-world image data of plank & pipe stacks with well distributed data
  • Annotated datasets to label individual planks and pipes
  • Trained object detection model for accurate detection & counting
  • Developed a mobile application to capture or upload stack images
  • Detected and counted each plank/pipe to compute the total count

Tech Stack

Computer Vision: Object detection, Tracking, Tensorflow

Backend: Flask micro service

Mobile Application: flutter & iOS based mobile app

Deployment: Azure

    Used robust object detection models trained on dense stack scenarios
  • Varying lighting and background conditions:
    Applied data augmentation, normalization during training & collection of diversed data
  • Different sizes and orientations:
    Included diverse samples to improve generalization

Impact

  • Improved inventory accuracy and operational efficiency
  • Minimized human error in bulk material handling
  • Enabled scalable counting across multiple sites

Future Improvements
