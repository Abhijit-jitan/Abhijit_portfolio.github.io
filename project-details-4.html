GenAI Led Personalized Medical Health Bot

Industry: Healthcare & Personal Medical Management     Technology: GenAI, NLP, Secure Data Systems     Deployment Type: Cloud + Mobile (Android & iOS)

Problem Statement

Managing 15–20 years of medical history along with continuously generated new health records for VVIP family members is a highly complex task. Medical data exists across structured, semi-structured, and unstructured formats including reports, prescriptions, vitals, appointments, and physician notes.

Fragmented storage, lack of real-time access, and dependency on manual coordination between doctors, nurses, and caregivers result in information gaps, delayed decisions, and increased operational overhead.

Objectives

  • Centralize long-term medical records with real-time updates
  • Enable secure ingestion of medical documents, vitals, and appointments
  • Provide personalized health insights for each family member
  • Allow GenAI-powered querying over personal medical data
  • Ensure strict access control for doctors, nurses, and support staff

Solution Overview & Approach

Solution Overview:
A secure, GenAI-powered personalized medical assistant platform that aggregates historical and real-time health data into a unified system, enabling intelligent querying, monitoring, and decision support.

Approach:

  • Ingested 15–20 years of legacy medical documents across multiple formats
  • Integrated real-time vitals, appointments, medications, diet, and exercise plans
  • Enabled doctors, nurses, and support staff to update verified medical information
  • Maintained individual medical profiles for each family member
  • Implemented GenAI-based querying for personalized medical insights

Tech Stack

AI & GenAI: Natural language understanding and GenAI models for medical document interpretation, contextual reasoning, and personalized responses.

Backend: Secure Python-based services handling data ingestion, role-based access control, medical record management, and query orchestration.

Web Application: Web-based interface for document ingestion, appointment management, and administrative controls.

Mobile Applications: Android and iOS apps providing real-time access to medical data, vitals, reminders, and GenAI-powered querying for family members.

Deployment: Secure cloud deployment with encrypted storage, controlled access, and compliance-focused design.
