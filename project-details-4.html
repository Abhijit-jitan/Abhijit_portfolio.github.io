GenAI Led Personalized Medical Health Bot

Industry: Healthcare     Technology: GenAI     Deployment Type: Azure + Mobile

Problem Statement

Managing 15–20 years of historical medical reports along with continuously generated new health records for VVIP family members is a highly complex task. Medical data exists across structured, semi-structured, and unstructured formats including reports, prescriptions, vitals, appointments, and physician notes.

Fragmented storage, lack of real-time access, and dependency on manual coordination between doctors, nurses, and caregivers result in information gaps, delayed decisions, and increased operational overhead.

Objectives

  • Centralize historical medical records with real-time updates
  • Enable secure ingestion of medical documents, vitals, and appointments
  • Provide personalized health insights for each family member
  • Allow GenAI-powered querying over personal medical data
  • Ensure strict access control for doctors, nurses, and support staff

Solution Overview & Approach

Solution Overview:
A secure, GenAI-powered personalized medical assistant platform that aggregates historical and real-time health data into a unified system, enabling intelligent querying, monitoring, and decision support.

Approach:

  • Ingested 15–20 years of historical medical documents across multiple formats
  • Integrated real-time vitals, appointments, medications, diet, and exercise plans
  • Enabled doctors, nurses, and support staff to update verified medical information
  • Maintained individual medical profiles for each family member
  • Implemented GenAI-based querying for personalized medical insights

Tech Stack

AI & GenAI: GenAI models, document interpretation, contextual reasoning

Backend: FastAPI, JWT based session id generation, Nodejs

Web Application: Nextjs

Mobile Applications: Android and iOS

Deployment: Secure cloud deployment with encrypted storage, controlled access, and compliance-focused design.
