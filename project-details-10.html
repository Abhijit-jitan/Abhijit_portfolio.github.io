Processed Rubber Bale Defect Detection

Industry:Manufacturing     Technology: Computer Vision     Deployment Type: On-Premise & Edge

Problem Statement

During rubber processing, granular rubber is pressed to form rubber bales or blocks. Due to uneven granule distribution, poor-quality raw material, or impurities, the final rubber blocks may contain surface-level defects.

Detecting these defects manually requires a trained human eye and significant inspection time, as many defects are small, subtle, and easy to miss in high-throughput production environments.

Objectives

  • Automate defect detection on processed rubber bales
  • Inspect all visible surfaces of the rubber block
  • Reduce dependency on manual visual inspection
  • Ensure consistent quality control at production scale

Solution Overview & Approach

Solution Overview:
A multi-camera, AI-powered inspection system that detects surface defects on processed rubber bales using object detection and segmentation models, ensuring comprehensive and reliable quality checks.

Approach:

  • Installed a multi-camera setup with five cameras covering all visible sides
  • Captured the top surface before flipping the rubber block
  • Used a mechanical flipper to rotate the block for full surface inspection
  • Integrated IR sensors to detect block presence in the inspection area
  • Triggered synchronized image capture only when a block was detected
  • Applied detection models to identify bale surfaces
  • Used segmentation models on detected surfaces to locate defects
  • Operators App to monitor the inspection
  • Dashboard App for Governance monitoring

Tech Stack

Computer Vision: Detection, Segmentation

Hardware Integration: MVS cameras, IR sensors, MVS Lights & mechanical flipper systems for synchronized inspection.

Backend: FastApi

Deployment: On-premise & edge device
